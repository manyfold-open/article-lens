pgvector is Now as Fast as Pinecone at 75% Less Cost

pgvector 跟 Pinecone 的效能比較直接打臉了「向量資料庫要用專門服務」這個主流論述。

A benchmark directly challenging the conventional wisdom that vector search requires a dedicated managed service.

💡 白話術語 Plain Language

理解這篇文章，你需要知道這 6 個詞 / 6 terms to understand this article

pgvector PostgreSQL 向量擴充套件 文章+留言 both
"pgvector 0.7.0 now supports HNSW indexing with comparable recall to Pinecone"

A free extension for PostgreSQL that adds the ability to store and search vectors—the numeric representations AI models use for text—directly inside your existing database. No new service needed.

一個免費的 PostgreSQL 外掛，讓你的一般資料庫也能存向量（AI 模型用來表示文字的數字陣列）並做相似搜尋。不用換資料庫，直接在 Postgres 裡就能用。

Pinecone Pinecone（專用向量資料庫服務） 文章+留言 both

A cloud service built specifically for storing and searching vectors. Easy to use, but you pay a fixed monthly fee just to keep it running—regardless of whether you're using it.

一個專門拿來存向量的雲端服務。好處是不用自己管，壞處是每個月固定要付費，不管有沒有在用。

向量帳單 Vector bill 留言 comments 非正式用語
"向量帳單比 RDS 還貴，這合理嗎？"

An informal term in this thread for the monthly cost of keeping a vector database running—even when you're not actively querying it.

這篇討論裡的非正式說法，指你每個月光是為了維持向量搜尋功能而付的費用，不管有沒有在用都要付。

HNSW 階層式可導航小世界圖 文章 article

An indexing algorithm that makes vector search faster by organizing vectors in a multi-level graph—like a map that lets you skip to the right neighborhood quickly instead of checking every house.

讓向量搜尋變快的索引演算法。像多層地圖——先縮小範圍到對的「區域」，再精準找到最近鄰居，比暴力掃描快很多。

ANN 近似最近鄰搜尋 文章 article

Approximate Nearest Neighbor search—finds vectors "close enough" to your query, trading a tiny bit of accuracy for massive speed gains. In practice the difference is imperceptible.

「夠近就好」的搜尋策略，不要求找最精準答案，換取速度大幅提升。實際上 99% 情況下近似值跟精確值沒有感知差異。

recall@10 召回率@10 文章 article

A benchmark metric: out of the 10 results returned, what fraction were truly the closest matches? 0.95 means 9.5 out of 10 were correct.

評估向量搜尋品質的指標：回傳的 10 個結果裡，有幾個真的是最近鄰居？0.95 代表 10 個裡有 9.5 個答對。

📝 文章摘要 Summary

pgvector 0.7 with HNSW indexing matches Pinecone's p99 speed at 75% lower cost, tested on 1M OpenAI embeddings. pgvector 0.7 加上 HNSW 索引，在 1M 筆 OpenAI 嵌入向量的基準測試中，p99 延遲跟 Pinecone 旗鼓相當，費用卻少了 75%。
  • HNSW index brings pgvector to parity with Pinecone on p99 latency at the 1M vector scale.HNSW 索引讓 pgvector 在 1M 向量規模下的 p99 延遲追平 Pinecone。
  • Cost comparison: pgvector on RDS ≈ $150/mo vs Pinecone ≈ $600/mo for equivalent workload.費用比較：同等規模下 pgvector on RDS 約 $150/月，Pinecone 約 $600/月。
  • Benchmark is single-tenant; multi-tenant or >10M vector workloads may differ significantly.基準測試為單租戶，多租戶或超過 1000 萬向量的情境可能有顯著差異。
